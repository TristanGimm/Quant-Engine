How do Hurst, ER and fractal dimension relate?

Hurst estimates how price changes scale across different lags. ER = |last price − first price| / sum |price changes| measures path efficiency: 1 is a fully directional path; 0 means no net displacement. A low ER alone does not establish mean reversion.

Fractal dimension describes roughness. D = 2 − H holds under self-affinity assumptions; a value derived this way would not provide independent confirmation. A separate Fractal Dimension Index is not used as a substitute for Hurst here.

Hurst determines the regime. ER and the OU fit do not override it. Short windows, held prices and market microstructure can bias the estimates.
